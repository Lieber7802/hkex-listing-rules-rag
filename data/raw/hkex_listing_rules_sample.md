Chapter 14A: Connected Transactions

14A.35: Disclosure Requirements

A listed issuer must announce a connected transaction in accordance with the requirements of this rule. The announcement must include:

(a) the names of the parties to the transaction;
(b) a description of the transaction and the assets involved;
(c) the consideration and the basis of consideration;
(d) the terms of payment;
(e) the maximum amount of the transaction;
(f) the percentage ratios as calculated under rule 14.07;
(g) the reasons for and benefits of the transaction;
(h) the identity of the connected person;
(i) the interest of the connected person in the transaction;
(j) the terms of any service contract; and
(k) such other information as may be required by the Exchange.

14A.36: Announcement Requirements

The announcement required under rule 14A.35 must be made as soon as reasonably practicable after the terms of the connected transaction have been finalized. In any event, the announcement must be made:

(a) within 3 business days after the date on which the terms are finalized; or
(b) if earlier, before the commencement of trading on the next business day.

14A.37: Independent Financial Advice

For a connected transaction that requires shareholders' approval, the listed issuer must appoint an independent financial adviser to advise the independent shareholders on the terms of the transaction. The independent financial adviser must:

(a) be independent of the listed issuer and its connected persons;
(b) have appropriate qualifications and experience;
(c) provide a written opinion on whether the terms are fair and reasonable; and
(d) state the reasons for its opinion.

Chapter 14: Notifiable Transactions

14.26: Size Tests

For the purposes of this Chapter, the size tests are calculated by reference to the following percentage ratios:

(a) the ratio of the total consideration to the market capitalization of the listed issuer;
(b) the ratio of the total assets to be acquired to the total assets of the listed issuer;
(c) the ratio of the net profits to be acquired to the net profits of the listed issuer;
(d) the ratio of the consideration shares to the issued share capital; and
(e) the ratio of the net assets to be acquired to the net assets of the listed issuer.

14.27: Classification of Transactions

Transactions are classified according to the highest percentage ratio:

(a) Very Substantial Acquisition: 100% or more
(b) Very Substantial Disposal: 75% or more
(c) Major Transaction: 50% or more but less than 100% (acquisition) or 75% (disposal)
(d) Discloseable Transaction: 25% or more but less than 50%
(e) Share Transaction: 5% or more but less than 25% (acquisition only)
(f) De minimis: less than 5%

14.28: Disclosure Obligations

For a discloseable transaction, the listed issuer must:

(a) announce the transaction as soon as reasonably practicable;
(b) publish an announcement containing the information specified in rule 14.29; and
(c) include details in its next annual report.
